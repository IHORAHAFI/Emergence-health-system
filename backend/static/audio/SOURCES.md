# Audio sources

## police_wail_emergency_siren.mp3

- **Original:** "American police siren in Washington DC" (`American_police_siren_i.ogg`, 29 s)
  - Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:American_police_siren_i.ogg
  - Originally from pdsounds.org (record 590), author "lezer", recorded 2007
- **License:** Public domain. The author released it into the public domain worldwide, for any purpose,
  without conditions. No attribution required (given here anyway).
- **Changes made for this project (2026-11-26):**
  - cut to the loudest 6.97 s (11.25 s - 18.22 s of the original)
  - 0.15 s fade-in and 0.37 s fade-out
  - loudness normalised (EBU R128, -12 LUFS, -1 dBTP), because the original recording is quiet
  - encoded as MP3, 128 kbit/s, 44.1 kHz mono
- **Check:** `python manage.py check_siren`
